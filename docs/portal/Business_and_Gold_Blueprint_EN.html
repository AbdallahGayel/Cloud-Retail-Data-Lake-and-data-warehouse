How Olist Marketplace Works & What Our Data Reveals

Olist is not a single store holding its own inventory — it is a Brazilian multi-seller e-commerce marketplace connecting small merchants (3,095 active sellers) with shoppers nationwide (96,096 unique buyers), orchestrating payments, carrier handoffs, and customer feedback. By combining store transactions with the B2B Marketing Funnel, our Lakehouse tracks the full loop from a merchant's first sales inquiry (MQL) to order fulfillment, cash collection, and customer review.

Why are there 96,096 unique buyers when silver.customers has 99,441 rows?
In Olist's database, every time a customer places a new order, the platform generates a fresh customer_id specifically for that order (creating 99,441 rows matching the 99,441 orders). However, customer_unique_id identifies the actual human shopper: there are 96,096 distinct buyers — 93,099 purchased only once (96.88%), while just 2,997 repeat buyers placed the extra 3,345 orders!

B2B Seller Acquisition Funnel — What Is an MQL?

Median Close: 14 Days
What does MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) mean?
Not retail consumers! An MQL is a prospective merchant / brand (B2B) that registered via a landing page or ad to sell on Olist:
1) 8,000 MQLs: Merchants expressing interest (Organic Search 2,296 · Paid 1,586 · Social 1,350...).
2) 842 Closed Deals (10.53%): Merchants converted by SDR/SR reps who signed an Olist seller contract.
3) 380 Active Sellers (45.13%): Signed sellers who actually fulfilled orders — leaving 462 contracted sellers yet to make their first sale!

What does Freight Ratio = 16.57% tell us?
Formula: Total Freight (2.25M) ÷ Total GMV (13.59M) = 16.57% — on average, shoppers pay R$ 16.57 in shipping for every R$ 100 of goods.
Why? Brazil is continent-sized, yet 59.7% of sellers cluster in a single state (São Paulo — SP) while 58% of buyers live across distant states. Long-haul inter-state routes inflate shipping costs, stretch average transit to 12.6 days, and directly drive the 8.11% late deliveries that crush scores from 4.29★ to 2.57★.
